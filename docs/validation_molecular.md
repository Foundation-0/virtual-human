Mean potential energy (a configurational average) agrees between the two engines within error bars, and does not drift with the time step: BAOAB samples configurations accurately even at 2 fs. Our kinetic temperature is read from full-step velocities, which BAOAB biases slightly low as the time step grows (a known property of the scheme); OpenMM's LangevinMiddle reports a half-step estimator, which is why it reads 300 K at 1 fs. The bias vanishes as the step shrinks (0.5 fs row). Error bars are block-averaged standard errors.
